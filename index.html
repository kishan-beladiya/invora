Questions?

Frequently asked

Is Invora free?

Yes — creating invoices & estimates, sharing PDFs and tracking payments are free and work offline, forever. Invora Pro unlocks 60+ premium templates, cloud sync, tracked links and automation.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Invora is offline-first — everything you create is stored on your device and works with no connection. Cloud sync, backup and shareable links are optional and only used when you sign in.

Which taxes and currencies are supported?

GST (India), VAT and sales tax — applied per item or per document — plus 30+ world currencies formatted correctly for your region.

Is my data private?

Your business data lives on your device by default. We don't show ads and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and cloud data at any time.

Can I use my own logo and branding?

Absolutely. Add your logo, brand color and fonts to any template. Invora Pro also removes Invora branding for clean, white-label PDFs.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play and can be cancelled anytime from your store account. Prefer a one-time purchase? Choose the Lifetime plan.
